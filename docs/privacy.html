Privacy Policy

SPS Search, its apps and the SPS Search New Tab extension · Last updated 17 November 2026 · Version 1

The short version. Searching runs in your browser, and your search words are not sent to us. Your settings and agreements stay on your device. Data leaves your device only if you turn on AI overviews, sign in, or submit a site or panel, and this page says exactly what is sent and where. No ads, no analytics, and we do not sell personal information.

1. Who we are

"We" means the operator of SPS Search and the SPS Search New Tab extension. Contact: open an issue on the SPS Search GitHub page.

3. AI overviews (optional, off by default)

If you turn this on, your search words and short snippets from the top results are sent to an AI helper service to write a summary: Cloudflare first, and if that fails, Deno or Vercel as backups. Those providers handle the request under their own privacy terms. We do not attach your name or email to it. Do not search for personal or confidential information while it is on. The summary can be wrong.

4. What is public

Please read this before you submit anything. Approved sites and knowledge panels are public: they appear in search, and their records can be read by anyone. Each approved record also stores the email address of the person who requested it, and that record can be read publicly. Pending requests are not public: only you and the admin can read them. If you do not want your email address stored with a public record, do not submit a request, or ask us to remove it.

5. The Extension

The Extension stores, on your device only: your settings (search address, background image address, shortcuts), search counts (a total and a count per day, never the words you searched), and a record that you agreed to the terms. It asks only for the browser's storage permission and cannot see your browsing history, tabs or bookmarks. It loads our logo from GitHub, and a background image only if you enter an address, so those hosts can see your IP address. It opens the Site when you search, and the Site section above then applies.

6. How we use data

Only to run the Services: show results, manage accounts and submitted sites and panels, keep things secure, and answer you. We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or build advertising profiles.

7. Who we share it with

Only the providers that help run the Services: GitHub, Google (Firebase, Firestore, Fonts), Supabase, and Cloudflare, Deno and Vercel for AI overviews. We may also disclose data if the law requires it. These providers may process data outside Australia.

8. How long we keep it

Settings and agreements stay on your device until you clear them or remove the Extension. Accounts, requests and approved records stay until you ask us to delete them or we no longer need them. Indexed pages leave search after the next crawl once a site is removed.

9. Your choices and rights

You can change your agreements in Settings, sign out, turn AI overviews off, or clear your browser data. You can ask us to show you, correct or delete your data, or remove a site, panel or your email from a record. Where you live you may also be able to complain to a privacy regulator (in Australia, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner). To ask, open an issue on the SPS Search GitHub page.

10. Security

We use reasonable measures, such as access rules on our database, but no online service is completely secure.

11. Children

People under 18 should use the Services with a parent or guardian's permission. A parent or guardian can ask us to delete a child's data. We do not knowingly collect more personal information from children than the features they choose to use need.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The date and version above show the current one, and you will be asked to agree again if a change matters.
